Privacy Policy

Effective October 7, 2026

Short version: RenewFold collects nothing. All your data stays on your device.

RenewFold is a subscription tracking app for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use RenewFold.

1. Data we collect

We do not collect, transmit, or process any personal data on any server. RenewFold has no servers, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party trackers.

  • Your subscription data (service names, prices, renewal dates, notes) is stored only in the app's local storage on your device.
  • Screenshots you import are processed entirely on your device using the app's bundled on-device OCR engine, alongside Apple's Vision framework. Images are never uploaded anywhere.
  • We do not ask for your name, email address, or any account. There is no sign-up in the app.

2. Permissions

  • Calendar: If you choose to add a renewal to your calendar, RenewFold writes that single event using write-only access. It never reads your calendar.
  • Notifications: If you enable renewal reminders, notifications are scheduled locally on your device.
  • Photos: If you choose a screenshot from your photo library, the app reads only the image you pick. It never browses or scans your library.

3. Purchases

RenewFold Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details or identity. Apple's handling of payment information is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

4. Data export

You can export your data to a JSON file at any time from Settings. The export stays under your control — it is only shared wherever you choose to send it.

5. Data deletion

Deleting the app removes all data immediately and permanently. There is nothing to delete on our side, because nothing ever leaves your device.

6. Children

RenewFold is rated 4+ and collects no data from anyone, including children.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
