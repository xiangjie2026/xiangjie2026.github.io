Getting started

Import a subscription in seconds: tap Quick Add on the Home screen, then pick a screenshot from your photo library, or paste the text of a renewal email. RenewFold reads the service, price, and renewal date for you. Review the details, tap Save, done.

FAQ

Does my data ever leave my phone?

No. RenewFold has no account system, no server, and no analytics. All data — including screenshots you import — is processed and stored entirely on your device. See the Privacy Policy.

What's the difference between Free and Pro?

Free includes up to 5 subscriptions, core import, basic reminders, and the renewal timeline. Pro (one-time purchase, yours forever) removes all limits: unlimited subscriptions and all import options.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Go to Settings → Restore purchases and sign in with the same Apple Account you used to buy Pro.

How do I move my data to a new iPhone?

On your old device: Settings → Export data to create a JSON file. On the new device: Settings → Restore from file. Your data never touches a server — it goes device to device.

Import says the screenshot isn't clear enough. What can I do?

Crop the image to just the subscription information (service name, price, renewal date) and try again, or paste the text directly via Quick Add → Paste. Low-resolution screenshots work best when cropped tightly.

How do renewal reminders work?

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. Enable them from Settings when prompted, and customize the reminder time on each subscription's detail page.
